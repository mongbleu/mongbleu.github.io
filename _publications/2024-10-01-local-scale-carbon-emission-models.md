**English summary.** Korean local governments are legally required to produce carbon neutrality plans, but the emission evidence available to them is aggregated far above the scale at which they can act. This paper develops spatial models that predict greenhouse gas emissions at 1-hectare resolution from energy statistics, land cover, building and road data, and evaluates how far the resulting surfaces can actually be used inside municipal planning — including where the downscaling breaks down. Published in Korean; the model is open source at [github.com/mongbleu/carbonBalance](https://github.com/mongbleu/carbonBalance).
