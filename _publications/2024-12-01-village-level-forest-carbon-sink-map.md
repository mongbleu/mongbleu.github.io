**English summary.** Forest carbon sinks are reported at province or county level, which hides the variation that determines where sink management is worth doing. This paper builds a forest carbon sink map at village level (*eup-myeon-dong*) by combining forest inventory data with land cover, and demonstrates its use for spatial sink management by local governments. Published in Korean.
