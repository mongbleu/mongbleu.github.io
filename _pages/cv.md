Research projects
======

**Crown Base Height and Crown Fire Prediction Module** · Korea Forestry Promotion Institute · Assistant Researcher, Apr 2024 – Dec 2025
* Developed a crown base height prediction module across 36 tree species as the basis of a crown fire simulation module
* Converted a physics-based fire-spread model into a differentiable CNN layer, constraining learning under sparse observations
* Produced training data from Sentinel-derived Normalized Burn Ratio (NBR) and observed GIS fireline data
* *Outcomes:* journal article under major revision (Ecological Informatics, first author) · patent KR 10-2025-0189148 · open dataset and trained model (Zenodo DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21736584) · KSFS 2025 poster (Best Poster Award)

**Local-Scale Carbon Balance Model and Maps of the Republic of Korea** · Environmental GIS/RS Lab, Korea University · Assistant Researcher, Sep 2022 – Aug 2024
* Built an AI-driven spatial model predicting greenhouse gas emissions at 1-hectare resolution
* Developed local-scale carbon balance typology maps for local government carbon neutrality planning
* *Outcomes:* 2 articles in J. Climate Change Research (2024, first author on one) · 1 article under review at J. Korean Society of Forest Science (first author) · M.S. thesis · open model repository ([carbonBalance](https://github.com/mongbleu/carbonBalance)) · EGU 2024, AGU 2023, KSCCR 2022

**Climate Technology International Cooperation Strategy Map** · Ministry of Science and ICT · NIGT · Researcher, Jan 2026 – Dec 2026
* Designed the analytical framework for the strategy map and its platform, covering cooperation with both developing and developed countries
* *Outcomes:* public platform deployed ([ctis.re.kr/map](https://ctis.re.kr/map)) · internal version for developing countries · 1 article under review at J. Climate Change Research (first author)

**SWAT-ABM Coupled Model for the Food–Water–Biodiversity Nexus (fairSTREAM)** · National Research Foundation of Korea · IIASA Strategic Initiatives · Assistant Researcher, Sep 2021 – Sep 2024
* Coupled a process-based hydrological model with an NSGA-II behavioral model, simulating one million farming households under climate and management scenarios
* *Outcomes:* manuscript in preparation (first author) · EGU 2025 and EGU 2023, both oral

**Land Cover Mapping for the National Greenhouse Gas Inventory of Korean Ecosystem Types** · Ministry of Environment · Assistant Researcher, Oct 2024 – Present
* Developing a nationwide land cover map using a U-Net model on satellite imagery
* *Outcomes:* 1 article in Ecological Informatics (2024, fourth author)

**Carbon Neutral Village Model for Rural Nepal** · UN ESCAP · Assistant Researcher, Sep 2024 – Feb 2025
* Designed the carbon neutral village concept and action plan; estimated mitigation and adaptation outcomes from field data collected in rural Nepal
* *Outcomes:* UN ESCAP report (2025), [hdl.handle.net/20.500.12870/8214](https://hdl.handle.net/20.500.12870/8214)

**Samoa Forest Carbon Detection and Quantification** · National Institute of Green Technology · Assistant Researcher, Apr 2023 – Jan 2024
* Developed ML- and DL-driven land cover classification and a satellite imagery processing pipeline

**National GHG Inventory of the LULUCF Settlement Sector** · Korea Agency for Infrastructure Technology Advancement · Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport · Assistant Researcher, Apr 2020 – Dec 2024
