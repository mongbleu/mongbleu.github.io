**Status:** deployed and public · internal version for developing countries deployed · manuscript under review

## What it does

Choosing which country to cooperate with on which climate technology is normally a qualitative, committee-driven decision. This platform makes it a data problem: it scores **cooperation potential** between Korea and partner countries by combining technology capability, national needs, and absorptive capacity, and presents the result as an interactive map that a policy officer can actually use.

I designed the analytical framework behind the map — the indicator structure, the scoring, and how the developing-country and developed-country cases differ — and its translation into the platform.

## Inputs and outputs

| | |
|---|---|
| **Inputs** | National technology capability indicators; NDC and technology needs assessment data; R&D and patent statistics; socio-economic and capacity indicators |
| **Outputs** | Country × technology cooperation potential scores; interactive strategy map; partner shortlists by technology sector |
| **Versions** | Public deployment for R&D cooperation with developed countries; internal deployment for developing countries |
